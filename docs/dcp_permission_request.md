**๓. ข้อหารือเกี่ยวกับการเผยแพร่ผลลัพธ์**

ดิฉันมีความตั้งใจจะเผยแพร่ **เฉพาะข้อมูลที่ผ่านการประมวลผลแล้ว** คือรายชื่อวัตถุดิบที่จัดมาตรฐานแล้ว
พร้อมชื่อจังหวัด (ไม่มีเนื้อความจากเอกสาร ไม่มีข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล) เป็นชุดข้อมูลเปิดเพื่อการศึกษา
พร้อมอ้างอิงถึงกรมส่งเสริมวัฒนธรรมเป็นแหล่งที่มา จึงขอเรียนถามว่าการดำเนินการเช่นนี้

แต่ใช้เครื่องมือปัญญาประดิษฐ์ช่วย *อ่าน* เอกสารเพื่อดึงชื่อวัตถุดิบออกมาเท่านั้น
อย่างไรก็ดี ดิฉันเห็นว่าควรเรียนถามให้ชัดเจนก่อน มากกว่าจะตีความเอาเอง

  ซึ่งอาจเป็นประโยชน์ต่อการวางแผนงานส่งเสริมของกรม

## English version — for the record, not for sending

**Subject:** Permission to use provincial food documentation for non-commercial academic research

Dear Director / Programme Officer, Department of Cultural Promotion,

I am [name], a Grade 11 student at International School Bangkok. I am carrying out an
independent data-science research project on **the geography of Thai cuisine** — studying
how ingredients and methods differ between provinces, and which provinces' local food is
least visible on the public internet.

I found the provincial menu documents the Department publishes at `food.culture.go.th`.
They are unusually valuable because every record carries an explicit province and all 77
provinces are represented. I am writing to ask permission for the following.

**1. What I am asking to do.** Download the PDF documents under `/food68/` (approximately
231), at no more than one request per second, with my project and contact email
identified in every request. Extract only the dish name, province, dish category and
ingredient list into a table for statistical analysis.

**2. What I will not do.** I will not store or publish any personal data appearing in the
documents — informant names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, or
photographs of people. These are discarded at the parsing stage, before anything is
written to the database, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562. I will
not republish the documents or their full text in any form. There is no commercial use.

**3. A question about publishing results.** I intend to publish only *derived* data —
normalised ingredient names with province labels, containing no document text and no
personal data — as an open dataset for educational use, citing the Department as the
source. May I ask whether this is acceptable? If not, I am glad to use the material for
analysis only and publish nothing derived from it.

**4. A question about robots.txt.** I checked the site's `robots.txt`. It permits access,
but also carries the signal `ai-train=no`. I want to be clear that **this project does not
train or fine-tune any AI model** on your content; it uses an AI tool to *read* the
documents and pull out ingredient names. Even so, it seemed better to ask than to
interpret this on my own. Would this use be within what the Department intends?

**5. What I can offer in return.** I will send the Department the resulting analysis and
map free of charge — in particular the finding of **which provinces have the least local
food documentation available online**, which may be useful for the Department's own
promotion planning. The Department will be cited in the academic paper and in every
version of the dataset.

I am happy to provide further detail or documentation on request. If the Department would
prefer I not use this material, I will stop immediately.
