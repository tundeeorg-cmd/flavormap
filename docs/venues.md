## What this changes, for the researcher to decide

None of these is decided here. Each is a planning or methods call.

1. **ICWSM needs a DOI by 15 Jan 2027.** The plan has the datasets public "by 15 Jan
   2027" on HuggingFace. ICWSM needs a DOI as well, such as a Zenodo deposit, and it
   must exist **at submission**. Whatever HD-3 settles about DCP redistribution also
   decides what that deposit may contain.
2. **The papers overlap in review windows.** ICWSM and JCSSE both refuse a paper that
   is under review elsewhere. The DH2027 short paper is under review until February 2027,
   and the ICWSM and JCSSE deadlines (15 and 22 Jan) fall inside that window. The plan
   gives each paper a different contribution (§2). Whether they are different enough,
   and whether DH2027's Book of Abstracts counts as publication, is a judgement call,
   possibly one to put to the organisers.
3. **JCSSE is double-blind and DH2027 is open.** The JCSSE paper must not link the
   public repository or the dataset. The DH2027 paper names the author and the repository.
4. **Questions to send the organisers.** Only the researcher can send these:
   - All three: the rules for an author under 18, including registration, copyright
     transfer (AAAI) and attending in person.
   - JCSSE: the deadline timezone, 3–6 versus 4–6 pages, whether the student rate counts
     as the paper's registration, and remote presentation.
   - DH2027: whether a declined short paper can become a poster (or check ConfTool).
